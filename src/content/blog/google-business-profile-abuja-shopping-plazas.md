## Why Google Suspends Multi-Tenant Plaza Listings in Abuja

Google’s verification algorithms were engineered under the assumption of Western urban structures: individual buildings with distinct street numbers. 

When applied to an Abuja plaza with 80 separate shops:
1. **The Shared Street Address Problem:** If 30 electronics stores all list `Plot 751 Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse 2, Abuja` without clear sub-identifiers, Google's automated fraud detection flags the newest entries as spam or duplicate attempts to game map rankings.
2. **Lack of Permanent Storefront Signage:** Google strictly prohibits paper printouts or makeshift vinyl banners. If your shop sign looks temporary, automated reviewers conclude you are an unauthorized sub-tenant.
3. **Competing Primary Categories:** When multiple stores in the exact same building register under the identical primary category (e.g., *"Phone Repair Service"* or *"Boutique"*), Google suppresses all but the highest-authority profile.

## How to Format Your Plaza Address to Pass Google Review

To prevent duplicate address flags, follow this strict address formatting architecture during registration:

### 1. Structure Line 1 and Line 2 Distinctly
* **Street Address (Line 1):** Enter only the official plot and street name.
  * *Example:* `Plot 751 Aminu Kano Crescent`
* **Suite / Unit / Floor (Line 2):** Enter your shop number and building wing explicitly.
  * *Correct:* `Shop B12, First Floor, Banex Plaza Wing B`
  * *Incorrect:* `Banex Plaza Wuse 2` (Too vague, triggers automated duplicate flag).

### 2. Match Your Permanent Exterior Sign
Your registered business name on Google must match the exact lettering on your physical signboard hanging outside your shop door. 
* Do not add descriptive slogans or phone numbers to your business name field.
* If your physical signboard says *"Kemi Hair Studio"*, do not register as *"Kemi Hair Studio Banex Plaza Best Wuse Stylist"*. Doing so will trigger an immediate suspension.

### 3. Maintain Supporting Tenancy Documentation
Before you submit your registration, ensure you have:
* A valid shop tenancy agreement or lease receipt clearly stating your business name and exact shop number.
* A utility receipt or plaza management service charge invoice under your name or company.
* Your CAC registration certificate showing your Abuja operation.

If Google’s automated reviewer requires manual documentation, having these ready ensures your profile is approved within 48 hours instead of languishing for weeks.

1. **Start from the Plaza Courtyard or Street:** Film the main plaza entrance and the official plaza signboard showing the building name and street location.
2. **Continuous Walk to Your Shop:** Walk without cutting the video through the corridor up to your specific shop unit.
