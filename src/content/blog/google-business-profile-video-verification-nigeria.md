# How to Pass Google Business Profile Video Verification in Nigeria (First-Time Approval)

If you have tried to register or update a Google Business Profile for your Nigerian business recently, you have likely encountered Google's mandatory **Continuous Video Verification** requirement.

For years, Nigerian businesses waited months for physical verification postcards via NIPOST that almost never arrived. While switching to video verification eliminates the postal delay, Google’s automated review systems are extremely strict. 

Over 60% of first-time video recordings by Nigerian business owners are rejected due to blurry signage, broken continuous takes, missing location cues, or GPS inaccuracies.

This guide provides the exact pre-flight checklist and recording protocol required to pass Google's video verification on your first attempt.

---

## Why Google Enforces Video Verification in Nigeria

Google's primary objective with Google Maps is fraud prevention. In emerging digital markets like Nigeria, bad actors historically created fake listings using residential addresses or vacant plots to manipulate search rankings.

Google’s automated AI and human review teams now require a single, continuous, unedited video that conclusively establishes three distinct criteria:

1. **Physical Existence & Precise Location:** Proving your business exists at the exact physical street coordinates entered into your profile.
2. **Access & Ownership:** Proving you have authorized, unfettered access to non-public areas of the business (e.g., unlocking the main entrance).
3. **Legitimate Operational Proof:** Proving active daily operations with inventory, point-of-sale systems, or business equipment.

---

## Pre-Recording Checklist: 5 Things to Prepare First

Do not click "Start Recording" on your phone until every item on this checklist is in place:

1. **Permanent Storefront Signage:** Paper sheets or temporary cloth banners are flagged as non-permanent and rejected. Ensure your primary signboard clearly displays your registered business name.
2. **High-Accuracy GPS Enabled:** Open Google Maps on your phone before starting. Ensure the blue dot accurately pinpoints your exact building. If your phone's GPS is drifting, restart location services.
3. **Physical Keys in Hand:** Have the physical key to your storefront or office door in your hand, ready to insert into the lock.
4. **Clean Operational Workspace:** Ensure your checkout counter, POS terminal, staff workstations, and business certificates on the wall are well-lit and clearly visible.
5. **Stable Mobile Connection (MTN / Airtel / Wi-Fi):** Video recordings fail to upload or get corrupted if your connection drops mid-upload. Stand where you have strong 4G or 5G coverage.

## The 3-Step Continuous Recording Protocol (1 to 2 Minutes)

> ⚠️ **Critical Rule:** The video must be **one continuous, unbroken shot**. You cannot pause, cut, edit, or stitch clips together. If you pause, Google's system automatically invalidates the submission.

### Step 1: The Exterior Streetshot (15–20 Seconds)
* Stand outside on the public street or parking area.
* Slowly pan your camera across neighboring businesses and permanent street signs to anchor your location context.
* Zoom in smoothly on your exterior signboard, making sure the business name and building/plot number are razor-sharp.

### Step 2: The Door Unlock & Entry (15–20 Seconds)
* Walk continuously toward your shop or office entrance without cutting the camera.
* Point the camera at the lock mechanism as you insert your key, unlock the door, and push it open.
* Step inside the premises. This single action is Google's primary test of physical tenancy and authorization.

### Step 3: Interior Operations & Proof of Commerce (30–45 Seconds)
* Walk behind the service counter or into the management office.
* Point your camera at your Point-of-Sale (POS) terminal, cash register, or payment barcode.
* Show business tools, branded packaging, staff working, or machinery.
* Briefly hold the camera still on any official documentation displayed on the wall (e.g., CAC certificate, local council permits, tax receipts).
* Stop the recording and submit immediately.

## What to Do If Your Video Verification Is Rejected

If your video is reviewed and rejected, do not immediately resubmit the same video. Repeating the same mistake can trigger a permanent suspension of your profile.

1. **Check for Address Mismatches:** Does the address on your profile match the exterior building plot exactly?
2. **Review Signage Lettering:** Did you add extra marketing slogans to your Google profile name that do not appear on your physical sign? If so, remove them immediately.
3. **Request Live Video Call or Support Appeal:** If automated video verification fails repeatedly, you can request a live interactive video call with a Google verification representative. In a live call, the agent will ask you to show specific documents, inventory, and utility bills in real-time.
