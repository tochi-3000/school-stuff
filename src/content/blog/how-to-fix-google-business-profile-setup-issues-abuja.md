## 1. Video Verification Rejected or Stuck in "Processing"

Since physical NIPOST postcards rarely arrive in Abuja, Google routes nearly all registrations in the FCT through **Continuous Video Verification**. 

Many merchants submit their 2-minute video only to see it rejected within 48 hours or remain stuck in "Processing" for weeks.

### Why It Fails:
1. **Broken Continuous Shot:** You paused, cut, or stitched video clips together. Google's AI reviewer automatically rejects any non-continuous file.
2. **GPS Drift:** Your smartphone's GPS was drifting when you initiated the video, placing your recording coordinates 300 meters away from your registered address.
3. **No Key / Possession Proof:** You filmed your shop from the outside but failed to show a physical key unlocking the door.

### The Fix:
* **Pre-Check Your GPS:** Open Google Maps on your mobile phone before starting the recording. Ensure the blue dot is centered exactly on your building.
* **Execute the 3-Step Continuous Flow:**
  1. *Exterior (15s):* Start on the street. Slowly capture the street sign, neighboring buildings, and your permanent storefront signboard.
  2. *The Unlock (15s):* Walk to your door without cutting the camera. Film your hand inserting the physical key into the lock and opening the door.
  3. *Internal Operations (30s):* Walk inside. Film your POS machine, branded packaging, inventory on the shelves, and business registration certificate (CAC) on the wall.
* **If You Are Rejected Twice:** Do not record a third video blindly. Request an appeal through the Google Business Profile Help Center to qualify for a **Live Video Call** with a human support representative.

## 2. Nigerian Phone Number Rejected or Not Showing

Another frequent issue in Abuja is entering an active phone number, only for Google to flag it with: *"Phone number pending review"* or simply refuse to display it on Google Maps.

### Why It Fails:
Google's automated spam algorithms flag newly added Nigerian phone numbers to combat fraudulent call-forwarding schemes. If the phone number does not match public records or uses inconsistent formatting, Google suppresses it.

### The Fix:
* **Use Full International E.164 Formatting:** Do not enter `0810 005 3846`. Enter `+234 810 005 3846` with the international country code.
* **Establish Cross-Platform Consistency:** Google crawls the wider web to verify that your phone number actually belongs to your business. Ensure the exact same phone number is visible on your website, Facebook page, Instagram bio, and physical storefront signboard.
* **Submit Signage Evidence:** If the number remains rejected, take a high-resolution, unedited photograph of your physical shop front showing the phone number clearly painted or printed on your signboard, and attach it to a support ticket.

## 3. "Duplicate Location" Flag in Shopping Plazas (Banex, Emab, Wuse 2)

If your shop operates inside a multi-tenant commercial complex (e.g., Banex Plaza, Emab Plaza, or commercial centers along Aminu Kano Crescent), Google's algorithm frequently detects that multiple businesses already exist at that exact plot address, flagging your new listing as a duplicate.

### The Fix:
* **Separate Plot from Suite Number:**
  * **Address Line 1:** Enter only the plot and street name (e.g., `Plot 751 Aminu Kano Crescent`).
  * **Address Line 2:** Enter your exact unit and floor location (e.g., `Suite B14, First Floor, Banex Plaza Wing B`).
* **Do Not Use Vague Plaza Names as the Entire Address:** Writing simply *"Banex Plaza Wuse 2"* without a plot number or shop suite almost guarantees an automated duplicate flag.
* **Have Your Tenancy Agreement Ready:** In case of manual review, have your signed shop lease agreement or plaza service charge invoice ready to upload.

## 4. Instant Suspension After Making a Minor Profile Edit

Many business owners get verified successfully, change one small detail (like editing their business name or tweaking their service area), and log in the next day to find their account **Suspended**.

### Why It Fails:
Google's automated fraud detection is sensitive to post-verification edits. Adding keywords to your business name that are not on your legal documents (e.g., changing *"Kemi Salon"* to *"Kemi Salon Best Braids Wuse 2"*) triggers an immediate suspension for keyword stuffing.

### The Fix:
* **Revert Name to Match Legal Signage:** Remove all promotional slogans, category descriptors, and phone numbers from the business name field.
* **File an Official Reinstatement Request:**
  1. Navigate to the Google Business Profile Reinstatement Form.
  2. Upload your official Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) certificate.
  3. Upload clear photos of your permanent exterior signboard and interior premises.
  4. State clearly that your address and operations are legitimate and compliant with guidelines.
